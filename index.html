Idle Bouncer is a casual idle/clicker game where your main goal is to maximize the number of ball bounces. Start with the Starter Ball that appears and watch your computer screen fill with bouncing balls and your points skyrocket. Upgrade your paddle, increase the number of balls, and improve your skills to maximize your profits and reach the top of the Leaderboard!.
1. Drop the Ball

Click on the board to drop a new ball. As long as you have balls available, you can keep dropping them.

2. Generate Money from Paddle

When the ball hits the paddle (yellow bar), you earn money. Hitting the walls doesn't generate anything.

3. Move the Paddle

Drag the paddle to move it manually. Click to toggle Auto-Move mode.

4. Buy Upgrades

Use your money to purchase upgrades

Add Ball : Add to ball stock
Ball Profit : Double ball value
Add Paddle : New paddle
Merge Paddles : Combine two paddles with the same value and side into one
Max Ball Stay : Increase the number of balls that can permanently stay.
Cloud Save & Synchronization

Create an account with email & password so your progress can be synchronized and continued on other devices, as well as displayed on the global leaderboard. Progress is still automatically saved on this device even if you're not logged in.
